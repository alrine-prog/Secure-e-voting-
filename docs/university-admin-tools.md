## Observer live tally

Observers can use `/observer/dashboard` to monitor their university elections and `/observer/live-tally?electionId=<id>` to view a selected election. The live tally refreshes every five seconds and reports approved voters, unique voters with a recorded ballot, voters yet to vote, total ballots, and vote totals/percentages for each candidate grouped by post. The API restricts observer accounts to their own university.
